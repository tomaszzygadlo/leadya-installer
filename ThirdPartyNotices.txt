LeadYa installer: third-party notices
===================================

The third-party components below retain their own terms and attribution.
They are not first-party LeadYa Apache-2.0 source.

The installer is built with NSIS and embeds the unchanged Unicode INetC and
nsisunz plugins. Their official sources, archive checksums and license
evidence are listed in DEPENDENCIES.md of the public installer project:
https://github.com/tomaszzygadlo/leadya-installer/blob/main/DEPENDENCIES.md
NSIS licenses: https://nsis.sourceforge.io/License

Earlier installer versions also embedded the ShellExecAsUser plugin with its
VistaTools notice. This version runs only with the ordinary user's token and
no longer contains that plugin.
